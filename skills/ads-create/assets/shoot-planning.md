# Shoot planning: turning concepts into filmable and photographable days

> Status: qualitative, unscored production aid. It converts approved hypotheses into a shot plan a photographer, videographer, or content creator can execute.

## Required gates

- Context gate: approved creative brief (hypotheses, hooks, claims), destination pages, placements, and the brand's visual tokens from the brand profile.
- Rights gate: model releases, location permissions, music and font licences, and drone or public-space rules are confirmed or listed as `needs_input`.
- Specification gate: capture formats (orientation, resolution, safe zones) follow the current official specification source IDs for the target placements.
- Review gate: the shot plan is reviewed by the owner before the shoot day.

## Idea generation (per hypothesis)

For every hypothesis list: the scene that proves the promise, the moment that carries emotion, the detail that signals craft, the environment that signals place, and the action that leads to the call to action. Prefer real service situations over staged tableaux; prefer showing over telling.

## Shot plan structure

| Scene | Location | Talent and releases | Shots (wide, medium, detail, movement) | Orientation and format | Serves hypotheses | Audio needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Capture every scene in the orientations the plan requires, capture clean plates for text overlays, record ambient sound and, where relevant, short spoken lines that can double as hooks.

## Day plan and checklist

Order scenes by light and travel, hold buffer time, assign one owner per scene, and keep an asset log (file names, scene, take, release reference). Deliverables: raw selects, an edit-ready folder per hypothesis, still frames for image ads, captions and transcripts, and a rights register.

## Do not

- Do not plan scenes that depend on unapproved claims, testimonials, or competitor references.
- Do not use people, places, or music without documented rights.
- Do not fix durations, counts, or budgets in this asset; they belong to the plan and the current specifications.
