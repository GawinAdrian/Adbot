# Audience personas: from observations to targeting inputs

> Status: qualitative, unscored research aid. Personas are hypotheses that must be labelled by evidence source and confirmed by the owner.

## Required gates

- Context gate: business model, offer, geography, price positioning, and the owner's description of real customers.
- Evidence gate: separate observed inputs (site content, approved customer data, first-party research) from inferred attributes; cite evidence refs per attribute.
- Regulatory gate: never build personas on sensitive categories or infer them for targeting; route regulated categories through `ads/references/compliance.md`.
- Capability gate: express targeting only in features that are currently available for the account and region, verified through current official source IDs.

## Persona record

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Name and one-line summary | who they are in the context of the offer |
| Situation and trigger | the life or business event that starts the search |
| Jobs, pains, desired outcome | in their words where possible |
| Objections and proof needed | what must be true before they act |
| Research behaviour | where they look, which words they use, who influences them |
| Platform presence | where they can be reached and in which mindset |
| Targeting inputs | keywords and intent themes, interest and placement candidates, geography, exclusions |
| Relevance | primary, secondary, or excluded, with the reason |
| Evidence refs and confidence | per field |

## Negative audiences

List who should not be targeted and why (not a fit, cannot be served, legal or policy exclusion, existing customers where appropriate) and which evidence supports each exclusion.

## Do not

- Do not invent demographic percentages, market sizes, or behavioural statistics.
- Do not target or infer sensitive attributes.
- Do not present personas as validated segments; validate through experiments and first-party data.
