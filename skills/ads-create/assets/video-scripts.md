# Video ad scripts: frameworks, shot lists, and handoff

> Status: qualitative, unscored creative aid. Durations, aspect ratios, safe zones, and text limits come from the current official platform specification source IDs, never from this file.

## Required gates

- Context gate: validated brand profile, persona, offer, approved claims, destination, and the placements the script must serve (vertical short-form, horizontal in-stream, feed).
- Specification gate: load only the active platform creative-spec references (`ads/references/*-creative-specs.md`) and record their source IDs in `specification_source_ids`.
- Rights gate: confirm rights for people, locations, music, fonts, and footage before a script names them; unresolved rights are `needs_input`.
- Review gate: scripts are hypotheses in the creative brief until the owner approves them.

## Frameworks (choose per hypothesis, never by folklore)

- Problem → Turn → Proof → Ask: open on the audience's felt problem, show the turn the brand provides, show approved proof, end with the eligible action.
- Story → Moment → Meaning → Ask: a real, rights-cleared scene; the single moment that matters; what it means for the viewer; the action.
- Show → Explain → Invite: demonstrate the service as it happens (reportage style), explain what the viewer gets, invite the next step.

## Script table (one row per shot)

| Time | Shot / camera | On-screen text | Voice-over or dialogue | B-roll and sound | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Rules for the table: the first row is the hook (spoken and on-screen), the last row is the call to action with the destination; captions carry the message with sound off; every claim in voice-over maps to an approved claim ID; name a music mood, not a licensed track, unless the licence is confirmed.

## Length variants

Produce one script per placement class the plan needs (short vertical, medium feed, long horizontal). Derive the exact maximum length and safe zones from the official specification for that placement and note the source ID next to the script. Cut from the longest version so hook and call to action stay identical across lengths.

## Handoff package

Storyboard outline (frames per shot), shot list grouped by location, asset checklist (existing footage, needed footage, graphics, logo, captions file), voice-over text as a separate block, and a review checklist (claims approved, rights cleared, specification verified, accessibility captions present).

## Do not

- Do not write testimonials or quotes that were not supplied and approved.
- Do not promise outcomes, guarantees, or timelines the offer does not carry.
- Do not embed platform limits, durations, or aspect ratios as facts here; resolve them live.
