# Funnel stages: roles, formats, and handoffs

> Status: qualitative, unscored planning aid. Stage roles are hypotheses; budgets, shares, bids, and thresholds are decided in the media plan with current evidence.

## Required gates

- Context gate: objective, conversion definition, customer journey length, and the account's measurement readiness.
- Evidence gate: stage roles and formats rest on current official platform capabilities and approved audience inputs; cite source IDs.
- Capability gate: retargeting, audience lists, and conversion features must be available and consented for the account and region.
- Regulatory gate: consent, data retention, and category restrictions are checked before any audience-based stage is proposed.

## Stage template

| Stage | Role | Audience source | Message job | Format candidates | Destination | Success question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | earn the first meaningful contact | intent or interest evidence | make the problem and the brand recognisable | video, image, search on broad themes | educational or portfolio page | did qualified people arrive? |
| Consideration | deepen understanding | engaged visitors and searchers | show proof and process | video, carousel, search on specific themes | service or comparison page | did they inspect the offer? |
| Decision | remove the last objection | high-intent searchers and returning visitors | make the next step concrete | search, remarketing, lead formats | inquiry or booking page | did they take the eligible action? |
| Retention and advocacy | extend value | customers with consent | invite reviews, referrals, add-ons | email, remarketing | account or referral page | did value continue? |

## Handoffs

Define for each stage how a person moves to the next stage (signal, list, or intent change), which stage owns each creative hypothesis, and how attribution windows and consent shape what can be measured; unresolved items are `needs_input`.

## Do not

- Do not assign budget shares, timing windows, or bid rules here.
- Do not assume every stage is eligible; verify features per platform and region.
- Do not treat platform folklore about stage performance as evidence.
