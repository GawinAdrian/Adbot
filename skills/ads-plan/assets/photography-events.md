# Photography and events paid-media planning questions

> Status: qualitative, unscored planning aid.

## Required gates

- Context gate: document the services (weddings, couples, families, business), the
  regions actually served, travel willingness, seasonality, capacity per period,
  objective, qualified inquiry definition, budget authority, and escalation owner.
- Evidence gate: verify current portfolio, service, region, availability, package, and
  review facts through approved inputs and current official source IDs; keep
  assumptions and stale evidence explicit.
- Regulatory gate: identify image rights, model releases, drone rules, music licences,
  consent, and platform policy on personal life events before proposing regulated
  activity or audience use.
- Measurement gate: define inquiries, qualified inquiries, consultations, bookings,
  cancellations, duplicates, and spam, and how offline bookings are reconciled.
- Capability gate: verify current location, lead-form, call, video, and audience
  features for the account and region before including them in a plan.

## Planning questions

- Which services and regions can the business fulfil in the coming season?
- What distinguishes an inquiry, a qualified inquiry, a consultation, and a booking?
- Which portfolio pieces, claims, credentials, and availability statements are approved?
- How far ahead do couples and families plan, and how does that shape timing?
- Which search, video, social, or remarketing roles are eligible and measurable here?
- Which exclusions are supported by query or lead evidence without blocking demand?
- How will inquiries, follow-up, contracts, and personal data be governed?

## Candidate considerations

- Consider intent capture on service-and-place themes, visual storytelling for
  awareness, and consent-based remarketing for consideration according to current
  evidence and capacity.
- Connect media recommendations to booked work and season fill, not clicks alone.

## Output guardrails

- Return qualitative options, source IDs, operational dependencies, owners, and checks.
- Do not prescribe radii, seasons, bids, channel shares, automatic account-action
  rules, or inquiry-cost targets.
